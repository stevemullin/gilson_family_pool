Picker Pill Palette

Every team's picker pill, both sides, shown on the pick card's white surface at the size it actually ships — 9px — and enlarged for judging. Home is loud, away is quiet, so two navy teams can never collide: only one side of any matchup carries colour.

Home

Team colour as the background. Text uses the team's other colour when it clears 4.5:1 — that's what keeps Steelers black-on-gold — otherwise white or near-black.

Away

Warm neutral #e9e4da with the team's primary as the text. Where that primary is too light to read, text goes near-black and the team colour moves to a hairline ring.

Once final

Both sides drop team colour entirely: the side that was right turns green, the side that was wrong turns red. Being right outranks identity.

Anatomy

The pill

  • border-radius: 99px
  • padding: 2px 5px
  • 9px / 700, Atkinson Hyperlegible
  • 4px gap between pills
  • Label is the picker's first name only — two-letter initials collided (Chantel and Christina both read “CH”)

The ring variant

  • inset 0 0 0 1.5px <primary>
  • Used on the 10 away teams whose primary is too light to read as text on the neutral: CAR, CHI, CIN, DEN, DET, JAX, KC, LAC, MIA, TEN
  • Background stays #e9e4da; text goes #2a2318

Layout on the card

  • Two columns matching the card halves above them
  • Away column left-aligned, home column right-aligned
  • Same divider as the card: 1px hairline, or the 58px state column when the game is live or final
  • Position carries which team — that’s why no abbreviation appears on the pill

Overflow

  • 3 other pickers per side, then a +N toggle
  • Toggle is the same pill shape, #efe9dd ground, underlined
  • Expanded it becomes less; one state per card, so either side’s toggle opens both
  • Roughly two rows per side at 390px

Your own pill

  • Navy #31527b, white text, always first in its column
  • Reads YOU +n where n is your points this week
  • Turns #256a3a green or #b03a2e red once the game is final

Same pill in the week grid

  • Identical treatment, but the cell shows the team abbreviation because position can’t encode it there
  • One 38px column per game
  • Logos were tried at 22px in the grid and 14px on the card; the card size was illegible, so pills won on both for consistency

Three changes are marked set by hand: Pittsburgh and Chicago now take what ESPN calls their alternate as the home colour — the gold and the brighter orange — and Philadelphia's primary is #076B3B. Everything else derives from the two source colours plus the rules above, so a change to a source colour re-derives both sides. Contrast ratios under 4.5:1 are flagged amber; nothing here falls below 4.24:1.
